Why effective altruism?

Good intentions.
Careful questions.

There are many ways to help. Some do much more good than others. Effective altruism is about understanding those differences and using what we learn.

At Michigan EA, we explore questions about global health, animal welfare, AI, and the long-term future. You’re welcome to bring a different view.
