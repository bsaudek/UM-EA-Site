Small decisions.
Outsized impact.

We're a group of Michigan students who ask a simple question — how do we do the most good with our time, money, and careers — and try to actually answer it, with evidence instead of guesswork.

Join the Intro Fellowship See upcoming events

Effective altruism isn't a cause — it's a question. Given limited time and money, what actually helps the most people, the most? We meet weekly to argue about the answer, then go try it.

What we do

Three ways to get past the theory.

Most of what we do is built around one goal: turning "I want to help" into a specific, well-reasoned plan.

01 / Fellowship

Intro Fellowship

An 8-week reading and discussion group covering cause prioritization, global health, animal welfare, and long-term risk. No background required — just curiosity and an hour a week.

02 / Speakers

Speaker series

Researchers and practitioners come talk about the work behind the headlines: what a malaria-net distribution actually looks like, or why AI labs hire safety researchers at all.

03 / 1:1s

Career conversations

One-on-one chats about high-impact paths — public health, biosecurity, AI policy, direct work, or earning to give — matched to what you're actually good at.
